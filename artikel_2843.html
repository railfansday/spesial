Jackie Cataline predicts BKB 61 TKO despite not studying opponent

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Jackie "TheHybrid" Cataline is preparing to face HaticeOzyurt at BKB 61 on Nov. 14 in London, England, and theAmerican has no intention of making the trip for a closedecision.Cataline is confident she can put away her Turkish-Dutch opponent,predicting a technical knockout in their upcoming bare-knuckleclash. The former wrestler, who has also competed in mixed martialarts and Power Slap, said she prefers to concentrate on her ownpreparation rather than study her opponents. Cataline also appearscomfortable embracing the role of the villain if the local crowdgets behind Ozyurt.

"I have not studied any tape. I don't do that. I don't look at myopponent," Cataline said ahead of her fight. "I just focus onmyself and what I plan on doing in the fight. And I plan on TKO'ingher. That's how I think the fight's gonna go."Cataline trains at Millennia MMA in Rancho Cucamonga, California, under coach Mark"The Shark" Irwin. While her background is primarily in wrestling,she has increased her mitt work and clinch sessions to prepare forthe demands of bare-knuckle competition."I feel like I'm in training camp all the time," Cataline said. "Itrain for MMA. I do jiu-jitsu. My training schedule is pretty set.I do add a little bit more mitt sessions in for the bare-knucklejust because I'm not a boxer."The 38-year-old also isn't concerned about potentially having thecrowd against her in England. In fact, Cataline believes that kindof atmosphere could work in her favor."I don't really care about the fans. And honestly, I thrive whenI'm the bad guy," she added.
