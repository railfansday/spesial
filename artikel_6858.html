Next-Gen Mineral Surfaces for Your Kitchen, Bath, and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Éclos by Cosentino merges natural beauty and breakthrough technology.

As a homeowner, the two things you tend to expect most from your ideal kitchen countertop are all too often in conflict: You want it to be truly beautiful, to evoke elemental, one-of-a-kind natural stone. But you also want all the resilience, innovation, and eco-friendliness that 21st-century technology can provide.

Renowned sustainable-surfaces brand Cosentino has made its reputation bridging those desires, and its Éclos line is both good-looking and good for the planet, simultaneously timeless in appeal and tech-forward in performance. That’s exactly what made it a surface of choice for many of the rooms in the House Beautiful Whole Home in Charleston, South Carolina, an effort of our sister publication. Here’s what makes Éclos the next generation of surfaces:

Unlike traditional stone that is cut from a quarry and installed in discrete slabs, Éclos is manufactured from a combination of minerals fused together with a proprietary technology the company calls Inlayr—the name of which gives rise to this new category of high-tech materials: inlayered mineral surfaces. Advanced robotics allow the surfaces to be imbued with sophisticated, three-dimensional veins and patterns that echo those of precious materials pulled from the earth. The result is the unique appearance of prized stone like marble or granite, with a strength that natural stone could never achieve on its own.

So what does that mean once you actually install Éclos in your kitchen or bathroom? For one thing, far less maintenance than might be required of natural materials. Éclos is nonporous, so it never needs to be sealed, much less resealed every six months to a year as, say, you might need to do with a marble counter. And it’s as solid as a rock—wait, more solid than rock: In the kitchen, it can withstand temperatures up to 428°F without showing signs of damage such as stains or cracks. In the bathroom, a knocked-over bottle of nail polish remover is no big deal. Dings, scratches, stains... things of the past.

One of Cosentino’s core values as a company is sustainability—the brand devotes a large portion of its research and development resources to discovering new and better ways to employ recycled materials and reduce its overall carbon footprint.

In the case of Éclos, Cosentino says the development of the surface involved nearly 30,000 hours of research and testing. The result is its most advanced product yet; Éclos uses zero crystalline silica and a minimum of 50 percent recycled materials (in some products in the new line, that goes up to almost 90 percent). That’s welcome news for designers and homeowners who are committed to incorporating sustainable, socially and environmentally responsible materials and practices into their renovations and new builds.

Of course, when it comes to creating a space you love and will be happy living with for years, state-of-the-artness only goes so far—style and sophistication are the other half of the equation. It’s a principle Cosentino, which also created designer-beloved modern surfaces like Silestone and Dekton, has doubled down on. The Éclos line is characterized by a carefully curated selection of quietly luxurious colors and textures, each crafted to mirror the richness and tactile appeal of quarry-cut stone. The surfaces have a visual depth and luster that almost make you forget how hardworking they are.

For kitchens and bathrooms—anywhere you can envision the sophistication of stone—Éclos can be key to crafting a space that is high-end but never high-maintenance. It's the future of surfaces, delivered right now.
