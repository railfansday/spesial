3 Best Movies on Prime Video This Week (Nov 2-6)

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Considering Halloween was just a few days ago, it should be no surprise that the number one movie on Prime Video this week is a zombie film. Specifically, it’s the new Spanish-language zombie action thriller Apocalypse Z: New World. Directed by Carles Torrens, the movie is a direct sequel to the 2024 film Apocalypse Z: The Beginning of the End. The movie has proven quite popular; however, it’s hardly the only thing worth checking out on the streaming service. Here’s a look at three great movies that we think you should watch on Prime Video this week.

A '90s Hollywood classic that launched Matt Damon and Ben Affleck to global stardom, Gus Van Sant’s Good Will Hunting is the inspiring story of Damon’s titular protagonist — a young polymath on parole who works as a janitor at MIT. When his talent is discovered by a professor, Will agrees to study under his guidance while seeking therapy for his troubles and confronting his demons. Robin Williams, Stellan Skarsgård, Minnie Driver, and Ben Affleck star as other key characters.

Astute and affecting in equal measure, Good Will Hunting has remained a critical darling since the day it premiered. While the storytelling is quite predictable, the movie is noted for its rich, deeply thoughtful narrative that is sensitive without being overly sentimental and can often be witty. The film boasts some of the most finely written characters, memorable dialogue, excellent music, and some of the most emotionally resonant performances of modern cinema, headlined by the stellar performances of Damon and Williams.

Directed by Gene Stupnitsky in his second feature directorial project, No Hard Feelings follows Maddie Barker, a 32-year-old, financially struggling woman, who takes an unusual job to save her family home. Maddie is hired by a wealthy couple to help their sexually inexperienced son Percy learn about romance before he goes off to college. Jennifer Lawrence stars as Maddie, Andrew Barth Feldman as Percy, and Laura Benanti and Matthew Broderick as his parents, with Natalie Morales, Zahn McClarnon, and Hasan Minhaj as supporting characters.

Part sex comedy, part coming-of-age rom-com, No Hard Feelings is raunchy, hilarious, and heartfelt all at the same time. The film was neither a major critical success nor a big box office hit, but it received generally positive reviews and is a very enjoyable ride. Critics have praised Lawrence’s comedic timing and dramatic acting in the film, which really carries the movie. Her performance won her a People’s Choice Award and a Golden Globe nomination.

Directed by Wayne Wang and written by Jeffrey Price and Peter S. Seaman, Last Holiday is a beloved 2000s rom-com starring Queen Latifah that’s loosely based on the 1950 British film of the same name. The plot follows Latifah’s Georgia Byrd, a humble store assistant living with unfulfilled desires, who sets out on a luxury tour after she finds out that she has a terminal illness and very little time left to live. The film also stars LL Cool J, Timothy Hutton, Gérard Depardieu, Alicia Witt, and Giancarlo Esposito in notable roles.

Last Holiday received mixed reviews and underperformed at the box office at the time of its release, but the movie has since evolved into a cult hit. The film has earned widespread praise for Queen Latifah’s incredible performance and ranks among her best movies. Charming, humorous, and very aspirational, Last Holiday is a warm, comforting film that’s sure to uplift your spirits this holiday season.
