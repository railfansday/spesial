Why this former TNA Knockouts Champion is still missing from WWE after 5 months

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Why this former TNA Knockouts Champion is still missing from WWE after 5 months appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Last seen wrestling in Jun. 2026 during an edition of WWE Main Event, former TNA Knockouts Champion Jordynne Grace has been absent since. A rising sensation in TNA, Grace joined WWE NXT earlier last year, before jumping brands to the main roster.

One of the most popular names on the roster, Grace has unfortunately been kept off TV for the last five months. While this raised speculation and rumors about her departing the company, Sean Ross Sapp has now crushed them.

According to Fightful Select, Grace signed a five-year contract with WWE in Jan. 2025 after she finished up with TNA. As per the report, her current WWE contract runs through 2029, keeping her status with WWE safe. However, while off TV, Grace restarted her passion for bodybuilding and started participating in competitions.

Recently, another rumor emerged that Grace was off TV to pursue bodybuilding. However, Sean Ross Sapp dismissed that, writing, "Regarding Jordynne Grace, she wasn't off TV to pursue bodybuilding, she was able to do that because she was off TV."

While her last WWE match dates back to a Main Event loss to Kiana James, her WWE TV match goes back even longer. Grace last wrestled on WWE TV on the Apr. 17, 2026 episode of Friday Night SmackDown, where she lost against Tiffany Stratton in a match to for Giulia's Women's United States Championship. Following that loss, she wrestled a few more times on non-WWE TV matches before eventually going on an unexplained hiatus. As of now, there are no confirmed updates on Grace's WWE return timeline.
