Personal Consumer Issues • Re: Which 2FA is the simplest to use?

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

But IMO, a place that wants to force passkeys ought to provide the necessary device.
But there is no device, other than what you’re using to access your account in the first place.
There is a separate necessary device if you access your account with a device that they don't like.

I use a PC - and only a PC - to log into websites. None of my computers have a TPM.

KeepassXC has passkey functionality throgh their official browser extension. But the site that requires a passkey of me does not support that implementation of passkeys. They want me to use a smartphone. I don't use a smartphone, and don't want one. If they want to provide me a smartphone to login, OK. If they'd also take Yubikey, they should provide one with the account. I don't want 2FA tied to a device that can be lost, stolen, or easily break. I'm opposed to vendor lock-in. etc etc.
