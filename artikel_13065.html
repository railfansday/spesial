What a wonderful, gratifying set of responses in this thread.

About 50 of you have weighed in so far. The vast majority of folks seem to have thought about this scenario, and have a solid plan. I don't see folks planning on "panic selling" or "slashing expenses".

Taylor Larimore, Mel Lindauer, and others who were instrumental in forming the Boglehead philosophy would be proud.......
Nobody here would say that they will sell stocks in a downturn in a panic way, but some might do that during a downturn in a calm way (if they feel they can time the market).
You did ask what I plan to do.

Though I plan to rebalance, here are two instances where
I did not rebalance during a market drop of 10% :

2020: At the start of COVID, the S&P 500 seemed to be oscillating
daily, sometimes by about 10% . I don't make quick financial
decisions. This goes with the saying "Don't do something,
stand there."

2022: Even though the market dropped 17% through the year,
the bonds that I had dropped by nearly the same percentage,
so no opportunity to rebalance.
So I just continued to contribute according to my AA, and
bought IBonds.
