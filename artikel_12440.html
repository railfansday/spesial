Rebecca Ferguson's New Fantasy Epic Officially Arrives on Streaming This Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Over the decades, we have seen our fair share of superstar actresses. In recent years, Rebecca Ferguson has become one of the biggest names in entertainment. Starring as Elizabeth Woodville in the British historical drama miniseries The White Queen in 2013 brought Ferguson international attention, as the role earned her a Golden Globe nomination. In 2026 alone, Ferguson featured in the divisive sci-fi blockbuster Mercy, which cost $60 million to produce but ultimately disappointed at the box office despite also starring Chris Pratt. Mercy would later go on to find major success on streaming, but Ferguson's upcoming 2026 blockbuster is also poised to perform a lot better at the box office.

Ferguson returns as Lady Jessica when Dune: Part Three premieres on December 18, 2026, later this year. Despite starring alongside Timothée Chalamet in what is arguably one of the most consequential modern sci-fi franchises, the actress is likely most recognizable in her role as Juliette Nichols in the Apple TV series Silo, which returned for its third season this summer. In the midst of all that has been happening for Ferguson in 2026, the actress found the time to hop on a brand-new fantasy project with Andrew Garfield, titled The Magic Faraway Tree.

The film premiered in London and New York in March and July 2026, respectively, in what was a film adaptation of Enid Blyton’s beloved classic first published back in 1939. After struggling at the box office, The Magic Faraway Tree is hoping to find some redemption via streaming the same way Mercy did earlier in the year. HBO Max is the home of prestige fantasy dramas like Game of Thrones and House of the Dragon. On November 27, the streaming service will welcome another fantasy entry as The Magic Faraway Tree comes on board. The film originally debuted to a flawless 100% from critics on Rotten Tomatoes, but currently holds a respectable score of 80%.

Four worlds and four completely different ideas of what a good life looks like. This is where you belong, what it would ask of you, and what you would get back in return.

You belong somewhere old enough that the mountains have names and somebody can still tell you who gave them. Middle-earth is for people who think the right thing is worth doing even when it will not be finished in their lifetime.

You want the sea, a crew, and absolutely nobody telling you where you are allowed to go. The Grand Line is the world for people whose main ambition in life is finding out what is over there.

You would rather be feared and useful than liked and useless. The Continent is a world where the monsters are the straightforward part of the job and the people are where it all gets complicated.

You want the myths to be real and loose in the actual world, and you want to find out at about thirteen that you were never anywhere near as ordinary as everybody kept telling you.

Directed by Ben Gregor, The Magic Faraway Tree was written by Simon Farnaby. In addition to Ferguson and Andrew Garfield, The Magic Faraway Tree also stars Claire Foy as Polly Thompson, Nicola Coughlan as Silky, Jessica Gunning as Dame Washalot, Nonso Anozie as Moonface, and Oliver Chris as Mr. Watzisname. The Magic Faraway Tree grossed $1.3 million in its stateside debut weekend, and its official synopsis reads as follows:

“A modern family relocates to the countryside, where the children discover a magical tree with eccentric residents. They’re transported to fantastical lands, rekindling their family bond through new adventures.”
