Not necessarily. If you don't need the new accrued interest feature, the easiest thing would be to just add the six monthly CPIs, May to October, from the table on this BLS webpage to the "CPI" sheet of your existing workbook. (I suggest copying and pasting to avoid typing mistakes.) Soon after the 8:30 AM release, the BLS should add October to the table.

If you do want to have the accrued interest feature, then you need to copy your holdings to the new workbook. But this isn't difficult if you're comfortable copying/pasting, deleting rows, and protecting/unprotecting sheets. Say you have ten TIPS listed on rows 4:13 of the "Interest&OID" sheet of your existing workbook. Follow these steps:
  • Make sure you have a backup copy of your existing workbook in case something goes wrong.
  • Unprotect the "Interest&OID" sheet in the new workbook.
  • Copy and paste from the "Coupon" to "Face" columns (cells A4:H13) from your existing workbook to cell A4 of the new workbook.
  • Delete the rows listing all the other TIPS from the new workbook (14:113).
  • Protect the "Interest&OID" sheet in the new workbook.
