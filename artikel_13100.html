Apple (AAPL) is slightly lower premarket, despite topping fiscal Q4 expectations after the bell Monday, as margin guidance caused some jitters.

On its earnings call, Apple offered up stronger-than-expected quarterly revenue guidance, due in part to continued momentum in iPhone sales. It expects revenue to grow between 7% and 10% year over year, above the 6% estimate.

But gross margins are expected to be between 45% and 46%, below Wall Street's forecast of 46.9%. Margins are being impacted by higher memory costs and some costs related to the ramp-up of Siri AI, Apple said.

SA analyst Agar Capital noted that gross margin in the most recent quarter enjoyed a 100-basis-point boost due to tariff refunds.

They warned a larger percentage of revenue increases are going to expenses rather than contributing to profitability, while some temporary factors are also causing margins to increase.

Highlights for the recent quarter included $57.58B in iPhone revenue, above Wall Street's estimate, while Apple's services division generated $31.57B in sales, about in line.
