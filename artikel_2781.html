Shohei Ohtani Extends Remarkable MVP Streak Despite Injury-Plagued Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Los Angeles Dodgers superstar Shohei Ohtani was an MVP finalist once again, marking yet another milestone of greatness for the Japanese import.

Ohtani was officially named one of three Baseball Writers' Association of America finalists for the National League MVP award.

This marks his sixth straight season as an MVP finalist, and his third straight with the Dodgers.

Ohtani is expected to finish second or third in the MVP voting, given that he missed time on the injured list and his hitting production dipped as he battled injuries.

All things considered, it was a great year, with a .275/.377/.512/.889 slash line, a 1.79 ERA, and 95 strikeouts in 14 starts.

However, he stopped pitching after early July, and along with his hitting slump, he totaled just 30 home runs.

Still, Ohtani posted 6.6 fWAR and 6.5 bWAR, with the potential to be much higher.

Pete Crow-Armstrong, meanwhile, delivered a historic season of his own, with a 9.8 bWAR and 10.6 fWAR.

He did it on both ends, hitting 45 home runs and stealing 41 bases while posting the best center field defense in the big leagues.

Ohtani has no chance of surpassing Crow-Armstrong, even at his peak, because Crow-Armstrong has the national narrative and the production to put him in a great spot to win Ohtani's long-held award.

Dave Roberts, back in August, acknowledged PCA's greatness this year.

"I've followed what Pete's doing and he's figured some things out with the bat," Roberts said. "He's having a heck of a year. He has rightfully made it a lot closer than most people would expect. He's having a tremendous season… he's done a lot of things with the bat that has made him viable versus left. Which has made him more of a complete player.

Now, an MVP-type season is on the verge of becoming an MVP-winning season, all of which will motivate Ohtani for 2027.
