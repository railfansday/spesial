Mechanics Bancorp (MCHB +2.80%) reported core net income of $60.6 million for the third quarter, focused on balance sheet optimization through the exit of noncore business lines and a restructuring of the investment portfolio. Management completed the sale of the runoff indirect auto loan portfolio and reinvested $308 million of available-for-sale securities into higher-yielding mortgage-backed securities to enhance future earnings. The company reported continued progress on cost synergies following the 2025 HomeStreet merger, contributing to an improved core efficiency ratio. Despite net interest margin compression driven by rising deposit costs, the bank maintained elevated capital ratios and confirmed plans for a $100 million dividend in the fourth quarter. Management updated forward guidance for 2027 to reflect a higher interest rate environment while noting significant embedded repricing opportunities within the legacy loan and securities portfolios.
