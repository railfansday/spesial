Man United and Chelsea told who's in pole position to sign Jacobo Ramon by sources

Manchester United and Chelsea are both keen to sign Como centre-back Jacobo Ramon within the next few transfer windows, sources have told Football Insider.

United view the 21-year-old as an ideal option to help reshape their defence, while Chelsea are keen as part of their ongoing efforts to bring in top young talent.

However, a move for Ramon is understood to be difficult for either club, with Real Madrid currently in the “box seat” to trigger their buy-back clause and re-sign their former starlet.

Sources say that the Spanish giants have the “best chance” to land Ramon, and are prepared to trigger their clause next summer if he continues to impress.

Jose Mourinho’s side are in the market for centre-back reinforcements in the coming months, and view Ramon as a strong option.

He left for Como back in 2025 and has since established himself as one of Serie A’s brightest young prospects.

Ramon is building a growing reputation for himself at Como, and recently scored against United in his team’s 4-1 triumph in the Champions League.

Cesc Fabregas has relied on the youngster at the heart of his backline, and he has been rewarded with some typically outstanding performances.

That has prompted Real Madrid to sit up and take notice of the development of their old academy starlet, who is going from strength to strength in Italy.

Their buy-back option could see Ramon return to the Spanish capital for less than £7.7million (€9m), such is the strength of his former club’s clause.

That will make it difficult for any Premier League club to win the race for his signature, given how insignificant a fee it would take for Real to sign him back.

Former United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider last week that his former club “will not rule out” selling winger Amad Diallo in January.

The Ivorian has struggled for game time at Old Trafford of late, and is continuing to attract notable interest from Aston Villa alongside a series of European clubs.

Brown believes that the price would have to be right for United to sanction a move, but Diallo is no longer seen as being protected within the squad.

He has started just four Premier League games so far this season, having struggled with injuries of late.
