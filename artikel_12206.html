‘Are You Even Listening?’ ‘He’s Been Extremely Complimentary’ — NFL Fans Call Out Claims of Troy Aikman Being Biased Against Bears QB Caleb Williams

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Some Chicago Bears fans were listening to Troy Aikman as closely as they were watching Caleb Williams in a 34-17 win over the Seattle Seahawks on Monday Night Football.

It came a little more than a year after his call of a Bears win at Washington set off a public back-and-forth with the Bears quarterback. That revived a perception among some Bears fans that Aikman is unusually critical of Williams (something Aikman has publicly denied).

One viewer wasted little time reviving that criticism during the game, accusing Aikman of questioning everything the Bears offense did. But to his surprise, others watching the same broadcast heard a very different Aikman.

Greg Gabriel, the Bears' former director of college scouting, wrote ahead of Monday's game that "Aikman does not like Caleb and has always been negative towards him."

Gabriel speculated that the perceived tension began after Williams and Aikman failed to connect for their usual production meeting before a game against the Commanders in 2025.

"Supposedly it started when Caleb missed a production meeting and Aikman took it personally," he added.

That meeting was scheduled for before Chicago's 25-24 win at Washington on Oct. 13, 2025, but the two never met. Aikman's critique of Williams that night, including his take on D'Andre Swift's 55-yard catch-and-run, drew objections from both Williams and coach Ben Johnson.

According to Awful Announcing, Aikman told "The Ticket," a Dallas radio station, that month, "I'm a fan of his; I like the way he plays."

Monday, Aikman was back on the call for the Bears game, with Williams under center. Early in the first quarter, one fan tried to sound the alarm, writing, "Troy Aikman has questioned literally everything the Bears offense has done so far tonight. I can't stand this dude."

Did everyone hear the same broadcast? You would expect so, but did everyone hear a bitter Aikman taking shots at the Bears and Williams? Not according to those who came to Aikman's defense, starting with one fan who answered, "Sounded fine to me?"

Others also shared his sentiment on the matter and waved off the complaint, writing, "Quit being a drama queen."

Two others heard more than a fair call. Another fan wrote, "Honestly, I feel like he has gone out of his way to compliment Caleb, which is a MASSIVE change."

Another fan made the same point, writing, "He's done a 180. Are you even listening?"

Finally, one fan was less diplomatic, asking, "[expletive] are you talking about? He's been extremely complimentary," before adding that they had muted the OP for "rage bait."

Whatever the booth made of it, Williams finished 24-of-34 for 247 yards, a touchdown, and no interceptions for a 101 passer rating in Chicago's 34-17 win at Seattle, and ranked 10th in Pro Football Network's NFL QB Impact Score with a grade of 78 for the Week 8 game.

The Bears, with the wind in their backs after wins over the Patriots and Seahawks, will now turn their sights to the Tampa Bay Buccaneers, whom they will host at Soldier Field for their third straight primetime game on Sunday Night Football.
