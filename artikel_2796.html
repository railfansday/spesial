Cubs extend Seiya Suzuki $23.15 million qualifying offer

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Cubs extend Seiya Suzuki $23.15 million qualifying offer appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Chicago Cubs extended a one-year, $23.15 million qualifying offer to outfielder Seiya Suzuki on Monday, giving the veteran a chance to return for the 2027 season while protecting their ability to receive draft compensation if he leaves.

Suzuki's 2026 production included 26 home runs, 86 RBI and 96 runs across 143 games for the Cubs. The 32-year-old posted a .270/.368/.473 slash line and an .841 OPS.

The productive season closed out the five-year, $85 million contract Suzuki signed with the Cubs, putting his future in focus as the offseason begins.

USA Today's Bob Nightengale included Suzuki among eight qualifying-offer recipients in his Monday report, the second-fewest in a full season since the system began in 2012.

"Chicago Cubs outfielder Seiya Suzuki: Final year of 5-year, $85 million contract."

Suzuki has until November 17 to accept or decline the offer. Accepting would keep him with the Cubs for another season at the designated salary. Declining would allow him to continue pursuing a longer agreement, either with Chicago or another club.

If Suzuki rejects the offer and signs elsewhere, the Cubs would receive a compensatory draft pick. Because Chicago exceeded the competitive balance tax threshold, that selection would come after the fourth round.

The offer does not settle Suzuki's future in Chicago. He and the Cubs can still negotiate a separate deal.

Chicago declined to extend qualifying offers to fellow free agents Ian Happ and Matthew Boyd, leaving Suzuki as the club's sole recipient this offseason.

The Cubs' next move hinges on Suzuki's decision, which will determine whether they retain the productive outfielder on a one-year contract or continue through free agency with draft-pick compensation at stake.
