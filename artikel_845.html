Suki Waterhouse Reveals What She Learned From Bradley Cooper Romance

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Suki Waterhouse, who has been with Robert Pattinson since 2018, recently revealed the lesson from on her two-year romance with Bradley Cooper and how it’s shaped her experience in the public eye.

Suki Waterhouse learned a bit about how to love, to love, to love in the public eye from Bradley Cooper.

The Daisy Jones & The Six star shared a rare look back at her first experience with a high-profile romance in her early 20s—she dated Cooper, who was in his late 30s, from 2013 to 2015—admitting she was going into loveland without a road map.

“When you’re younger, you go into everything so blindly and you don’t think of the consequences, including maybe of relationships,” Waterhouse explained in an interview with The Times published Oct. 31, not naming Cooper explicitly. “You don’t really have any concept of any repercussions of being in things that are public.”

In the more than a decade since, Waterhouse has built a life with Robert Pattinson—with whom she welcomed a daughter in 2024—and learned, “You can have a private life and a public life at the same time.”

And though she didn’t during her romance with Cooper, the 34-year-old shared, “I know how to separate those two.”

Once she met the Twilight alum in 2018, Waterhouse assured she was more than ready to go the distance.

“I can’t believe that I have this in my life and I can’t believe that I’m so happy,” she emphasized. “And maybe I was surprised because before I had only ever gone out with someone for two years and if it was two years it would have been a disaster at the end.”

Although Waterhouse is crediting her ability to keep her personal life private to her relationship with the 51-year-old, the “OMG” singer has always kept romance close to her chest.

“I don't talk about my boyfriend because it's boring,” she told Elle U.K. in 2014. "At least, that's what I decided to tell you when you asked. I do think whatever I say will sound weird. But the truth is, if I start talking about him, I probably won't be able to stop. And I don't really want to talk about him, you know?”

Waterhouse and Cooper dated from 2013 to 2015 and even made a red carpet debut at the Oscars in 2015 just weeks before their split was confirmed.

At the time, a source told E! News that Cooper——who went on to date Irina Shayk, with whom he shares 9-year-old daughter Lea, shortly after their split before beginning his romance with Gigi Hadid in 2023—wanted to start a family but Waterhouse was not on the same page.

“They remain friends but they both want different things right now,” the insider said. “Suki isn't ready. She loves Bradley and he loves her but she's so young and wants to concentrate on her acting career before becoming a mom.”
