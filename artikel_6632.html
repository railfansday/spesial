14 Desserts Made Better with Brown Butter

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

These brown butter desserts give your favorite baking recipes a lot more oomph.

The French have been making beurre noisette for centuries, and for good reason. Browning butter toasts its milk solids, creating a deep golden hue and a wonderfully nutty aroma. It only takes about seven minutes and makes a world of difference in dessert recipes.

This vertical spin on a cherished classic is packed with spices and sure to impress. The brown butter cream cheese frosting truly takes the vertical cake over the top! —Mark Neufang, Milwaukee, Wisconsin
