Seahawks activate Jadarian Price from IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Seahawks head coach Mike Macdonald didn’t commit to playing running back Jadarian Price against the Bears when he was asked about it on Saturday, but it looks like the rookie will be back in the lineup.

Price was activated from injured reserve on Monday afternoon and he had no injury designation for the game, so it would appear he’ll be active for the first time since Week 3. Price has been sidelined by a chest injury.

The Seahawks could also welcome Zach Charbonnet back to the backfield on Monday night. He was activated in Week 7, but did not play in Seattle’s win over the Chiefs.

Running back Robert Henry was waived to open a spot for Price, which leaves Emanuel Wilson, George Holani and Velus Jones to go with Price and Charbonnet.

The Seahawks also elevated wide receiver Montorie Foster Jr. and offensive tackle Bobby Hart from the practice squad. Wide receiver Rashid Shaheed and left tackle Charles Cross have both been ruled out for the matchup with the Bears.
