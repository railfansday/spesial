No Extra Lesson Plan: 6 Ways Preschoolers Can Learn During Daily Routines

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

From grocery trips to bedtime, everyday routines can give young children opportunities to practice language, early math, problem-solving, and independence.

GREENVILLE, S.C., Nov. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Families may not have extra time to plan educational activities between work, errands, dinner, and bedtime. But many of the routines already built into their day give young children opportunities to count, sort, make choices, follow steps, and talk through what they are doing. Tami Nix, president of Archway Academy and a past president of the South Carolina Association of Early Care and Education, says parents do not need to create a separate activity to make those moments useful.

"When children take part in something the family is already doing, parents can see what questions they ask and how they think through each step, allowing the conversation to develop naturally," Nix said.

Research has linked everyday actions with later development. A study published in Early Education and Development analyzed data from 3,250 children and found that stronger family routines during the preschool years were associated with greater gains in reading, math, and prosocial behavior as children entered kindergarten.

Parents can also let children take the lead. Rather than stepping in immediately, adults can let preschoolers work through a problem on their own. These everyday experiences can help children develop confidence in their abilities and become more comfortable handling challenges independently. "So often, a teaching opportunity can get lost in the daily rush. Sometimes the best thing a parent can do is give a child time to try," Nix said. "Small moments of independence are just as valuable as getting the task done quickly."

When Tami Nix had the opportunity to acquire a small childcare business in 1985, she saw the foundation for something more than traditional daycare. More than 40 years later, Archway Academy has grown into a multi-site South Carolina early childhood education organization serving children from infancy through four-year-old kindergarten (4K), along with after-school and summer programs. Archway Academy's approach focuses on preparing the whole child through hands-on learning, trained teachers, parent involvement, and age-appropriate experiences that support children socially, emotionally, physically, and academically. Its newest campus in Greenville extends that approach across approximately 4.5 acres of park-like outdoor space. For more information, visit Archway Academy.
