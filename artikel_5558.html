TrueLoyal® Appoints Igor Petrushenko as VP of Engineering

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Founder of the no-code platform QuintaDB will lead engineering as TrueLoyal changes how it builds and delivers software with AI

Petrushenko founded QuintaDB in 2007 and ran it for 19 years, growing the bootstrapped no-code online database platform from zero into a product serving customers across the EU in 17 languages. He owned it end to end, from the product and architecture to the code, servers, support, and financials. Over the last two years he rebuilt it around AI, including a production multi-agent system that runs the business day to day, features that generate complete apps and websites from a plain-text description, and tools that let AI assistants work with the platform directly. Through all nineteen years, his work has been translating between what a business wants and what a system can do, for customers who did not come to work to configure software.

He has been living it for years, and he knows how to bring the whole business along with him, which matters as much as the engineering."

"For nineteen years I owned a product end to end, and for the last two I ran it with a team of AI agents," said Petrushenko. "I'm not an AI evangelist. I measure what it returns, and every change an agent makes gets checked before we call it done. That's the discipline I'm bringing to TrueLoyal, so the brands we serve get what they need faster and can count on it."
