Todd Golden, Thomas Haugh talk Florida's season-opening win over Miami

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Florida basketball opened up the 2026-27 campaign with a 77-67 victory over the Miami Hurricanes in the Orion180 Tipoff Classic held at Benchmark International Arena — the same venue the Gators were knocked out of the NCAA Tournament last year by Iowa.

After the game, head coach Todd Golden and small forward Thomas Haugh spoke to the media bout their team's performance, discussing topics including the quality of Miami as an opponent, what Florida did well and did not do well, the performances of a few players in particular, plus a few other items of interest.
