While it's true that footwear trends are cyclical, there's one classic sneaker that's back in a big way, though it never quite left the trend cycle.

"Skinny sneakers" may feel like the new 'It' shoes in town, but their streamlined, low-to-the-ground shape has the staying power of a classic plimsoll. Jil Sander and PUMA’s latest collaboration brings the trend to the forefront, but we found affordable pairs that nail the trend for less, so you can wear them now and for seasons to come with a good deal.

Skinny sneakers, or "glove sneakers", acts as an umbrella term for streamlined, low-profile sneakers: think slim uppers, low flat soles and less bulk all around.

The low-profile shape traces back to the plimsoll, one of the earliest sneaker styles in the fashion cycle. Some fit close to the foot like a glove, while others take inspiration from “sneakerinas,” the ballet-flat-meets-sneaker trend.

Designer brands have helped push the silhouette back into the spotlight. Labels like Miu Miu and Louis Vuitton have helped popularize ballet-inspired sneaker hybrids, the ladder introduced its own LV Sneakerina in 2025, cementing the style into a high-profile luxury trend.

The latest Jil Sander x PUMA collaboration brings the brands’ streamlined aesthetic to Puma's trending Speedcat silhouette. The $500 Speedcat comes in black and white leather. However, there are plenty of other solid choices priced well below that.

Slim-cut sneakers from the likes of Alo and Vivaia have already been worn by countless stars thus serving as strong choices. Designer names like Tommy Hilfiger and Michael Kors are making the trend attainable with end-of-season deals. While active brands like Merrell and Adidas bring the trend to the great outdoors with functional, gorp-core iterations.

Their versatility helps explain the trend’s staying power: slim sneakers pair easily with jeans, trousers, skirts and dresses, and familiar shapes like the plimsoll tend to feel current season after season.
