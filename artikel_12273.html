Green Bay Packers’ upcoming Patriots game gets a familiar face for FOX Broadcast Assignment

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Green Bay Packers' upcoming matchup against the New England Patriots will feature a familiar voice in the broadcast booth. Tom Brady, who owns seven Super Bowl titles, is scheduled to join FOX as its top game analyst for Green Bay's visit to New England on Sunday, November 8.

Matt Schneidman of The Athletic confirmed the assignment on Sunday, November 1. He reported that Brady will work alongside Kevin Burkhardt, Erin Andrews and Tom Rinaldi for FOX's Week 9 broadcast.

The matchup will mark Brady's first appearance at Gillette Stadium as a broadcaster, bringing him back to his former home field in a different capacity. It also gives him a chance to assess Packers quarterback Jordan Love as Green Bay looks to gain momentum heading into the second half of the season.

Brady will serve as the game analyst, working with FOX's top play-by-play announcer, Kevin Burkhardt. Erin Andrews and Tom Rinaldi will cover the game from the sidelines.

The quartet is now in its third season together as the network's No. 1 NFL broadcast crew. Brady's involvement carries added significance given his ties to New England. He spent 20 seasons with the Patriots, winning six Super Bowl championships before adding a seventh title with the Tampa Bay Buccaneers.

Nov 17, 2024; Chicago, Illinois, USA; Former quarterback and current NFL announcer Brady looks on before the game between the Chicago Bears and Green Bay Packers at Soldier Field. Mandatory Credit: Daniel Bartel-Imagn Images

Brady's return to a Patriots game will come on November 8, when he joins FOX's broadcast crew for the first time as an analyst covering his former franchise. Green Bay and New England are scheduled to meet at Gillette Stadium at 4:25 p.m. ET, and the game will be carried on FOX One for streaming audiences.
