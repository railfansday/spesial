"It's Absolutely Still In Progress" - Yacht Club Shares An Update About Mina The Hollower's Physical Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The team is also working on "more post-release features"

Mina The Hollower has been digitally available for Switch 1 & 2 since May, and if you're wondering about the status of the physical edition, you're not alone.

During a chat with the Switch community on Reddit this week, developer Yacht Club Games has reassured fans it's "still planned". Company founder Sean Velasco says the team is working on finding the right publishing and distribution partner:

"Yes! It’s still planned. We’re working on finding the right publishing and distribution partner to make it happen. No release date to share yet, but it’s absolutely still in progress!

As for the physical version's release window, it sounds like fans can expect it by next year. Yacht Club has already released some Mina the Hollower merch via Fangamer and a soundtrack vinyl on iam8bit, and an art book is also on the way.

During the same chat, Yacht Club mentioned how it's currently working on "more post-release features" and "some quality of life and accessibility content updates to polish the full experience", while reiterating there are no plans for DLC right now:

"Nothing to announce on DLC right now, but we’re working on more post-release features! Expanded randomizer options, enemy and boss randomization, more modifiers, speedrunner features... lots of ways to do even more in Mina."

So, when the physical version of Mina the Holllower does eventually arrive, it will hopefully be the definitive version of the game.
