Advanced Energy Doubles Its Operating Margin: Rapid Earnings Reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The power-systems supplier is riding the AI buildout and turning it into real profit. Here's what's driving the margin gains and the one number that deserves a closer look.

Advanced Energy Industries (AEIS -2.19%) grew third-quarter revenue 50% to about $696 million, and GAAP earnings per share more than doubled to $2.80. Shares rose about 10% in early after-hours trading. Sales are now growing much faster than costs, so profit is climbing fast. The weak spot is cash, which is not keeping up with earnings.

This is operating leverage at work: operating expenses rose just 17% from a year ago. Research and overhead costs don't grow with every order, so most of the extra sales dollars fell straight to profit. A higher tax rate kept net income from rising as fast as operating income.

Most of the growth came from two markets tied to the same trend. Data center computing revenue rose 49%, on top of the chip equipment surge. Both depend on AI spending, since AI servers and the tools that make AI chips both need precise power. That makes the run strong but also concentrated.

The cost of growth is showing up on the balance sheet. Inventory has jumped 57% since December as the company builds for demand and ramps its Thailand factory. That ties up cash, and operating cash flow covered only a little more than half of net income this quarter.

Watch whether inventory starts turning back into cash next quarter. A second check is whether Q4 revenue lands in the upper half of guidance.
