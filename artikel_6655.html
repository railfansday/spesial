Slow-Cooker Barbecue Chicken

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

This slow-cooker barbecue chicken is as easy as it gets. Pile it onto sandwiches, grain bowls, wraps and more for easy meals all week.

Save time on busy days with this couldn’t-be-simpler slow-cooker barbecue chicken recipe. With just three ingredients and a few hours, the Crockpot does all the work while you get on with your day. Add it to your rotation of slow-cooker dump dinners for a satisfying meal that practically makes itself.

This tender, saucy pulled chicken is perfect for meal prep, and one batch can turn into several different meals. Pile it onto toasted buns with tangy coleslaw, spoon it over rice for an easy grain bowl, or load it onto baked potatoes with cheese. You can even use it to make nachos or wraps.

Chicken breasts and barbecue sauce are all you need to make tender, saucy pulled chicken. Add the chicken to your slow cooker, season it with salt, and pour your favorite barbecue sauce over the top. Cover the pot and cook the chicken until it’s tender, then shred it with two forks and stir it back into the sauce. Pile the chicken onto buns, spoon it over rice or enjoy it on its own for an easy, high-protein meal.

Place the chicken breasts in a 6- or 8-quart slow cooker. Season them with salt, then cover them with the barbecue sauce.

Editor’s Tip: Arrange the chicken in a single layer in the slow cooker to ensure it cooks evenly.

Cover and cook the chicken on low for six to eight hours or on high for three to four hours, until it’s tender and begins to fall apart.

If you’d like shredded barbecue chicken, use tongs to transfer the chicken breasts to a cutting board and shred them with two forks. Then, return the shredded chicken to the slow cooker and stir it into the sauce. Add more barbecue sauce, if desired.

Editor’s Tip: For a quicker way to shred the chicken, place the cooked chicken in a large mixing bowl and use a hand mixer on low speed. The beaters will shred the chicken in seconds.

Let the chicken cool slightly, then transfer it, along with any leftover sauce, to an airtight container or a resealable storage bag. Refrigerate it for up to four days or freeze for up to three months.

Reheat the barbecue chicken in the microwave or on the stovetop until it’s warmed through, adding a splash of chicken broth or extra barbecue sauce as needed to keep it moist. If reheating the chicken from frozen, thaw it overnight in the refrigerator first.

Slow-cooker barbecue chicken pairs well with all your favorite cookout sides! Serve it with baked beans, corn on the cob, potato salad, coleslaw, or mac and cheese for a classic barbecue-inspired meal.
