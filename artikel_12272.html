Jaxon Smith-Njigba maintains record-setting pace with 118 yards on MNF

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Seattle Seahawks WR Jaxon Smith-Njigba had six catches (on 11 targets) for 118 yards and a touchdown against the Chicago Bears in Week 8.

Why It Matters: With his 6-118-1 performance on Monday night, Smith-Njigba now ranks first or second in receptions (64), receiving yards (985), and touchdowns (9) on the season. Even with no other receiving threat for teams to worry about, JSN continues to deliver week after week -- he now has seven games of 100-plus yards this year with a 5-76-0 effort in Week 4 versus the Chargers the only game where he failed to reach the century mark. As long as he's healthy, Smith-Njigba should remain locked in your lineup as an elite WR1.
