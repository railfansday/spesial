Roku's Ads Drive a 7-Fold Jump in Profit: Rapid Earnings Reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Roku's latest quarter showed an ad business that's growing and getting more profitable at the same time. Here's what drove the gain, what flattered it, and what to watch while the Fox deal is pending.

Roku (ROKU +0.26%) earned $183 million in the third quarter, up from $25 million a year ago, as revenue rose 24% to about $1.5 billion. Shares were roughly flat in early after-hours trading. Most of that profit gain is real: Roku's ad business is growing fast and keeping more of each dollar. But lower marketing spend also helped, and Roku expects a big part of that cost to land in Q4.

Advertising did most of the work. Ad gross profit rose about $114 million, nearly all of Roku's gross profit gain. Roku credits a shift toward higher-margin ad products. Platform revenue also grew more than twice as fast as viewing, so Roku is earning more on each hour people stream.

Some ad strength is cyclical. Roku says political ads beat the same quarter of the 2024 presidential cycle. That money shows up in election years and fades in off years like 2027.

The cost side needs a footnote. Roku expects distribution costs for Roku TV models, a big piece of marketing spend, to be weighted toward Q4. The quarter also included a $22 million gain from ending part of an office lease. Neither changes the story, but both flatter this quarter.

Watch Q4 sales and marketing spend to see how much of this quarter's savings was timing. Roku gave no financial outlook because of its pending sale to Fox (FOXA -0.76%), so updates on that deal matter too.
