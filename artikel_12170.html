JJ Redick gives injury update on Adou Thiero

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Forward Ziaire Williams returned to the court for the Los Angeles Lakers on Monday when they defeated the Miami Heat 138-117 after missing their previous three games with a foot injury. He came off the bench, played 15 minutes and hit three of his six shots from 3-point range to score nine points while also chipping in two rebounds and two assists.

Rookie guard Cameron Carr was also cleared to play recently after missing the Lakers' first six games with an oblique strain. That leaves second-year forward Adou Thiero as their only remaining injured player.

Thiero has been out with swelling in his knee. Coach JJ Redick said that the hope is that Thiero will be able to return to live action by the end of this week, per Khobi Price of the California Post.

Thiero, who is 6-foot-7 and 220 pounds, is arguably the most athletically gifted player on L.A.'s roster. While his offensive skills are still rudimentary, he has shown an ability to get to the rim off the dribble, draw fouls, be disruptive on the defensive end and help on the boards.

He missed long stretches of action last season due to knee problems. He appeared in 25 games for L.A. and totaled 47 points, 28 rebounds and 10 assists in six minutes per game, and he averaged 15.4 points a game on 62.5% shooting during the G League regular season.

The Lakers will visit the Sacramento Kings on Wednesday before returning home to host the Portland Trail Blazers on Friday. They will then start a four-game road trip this weekend with a matchup against the Detroit Pistons on Sunday.
