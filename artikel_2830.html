Volleyball: Lady Irish ends Batesville season at semistate

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Regional champion Batesville headed to Jasper for semistate action Saturday to take on Indianapolis Cathedral. The Lady Irish eliminated the Lady Bulldogs 25-15, 25-18, 25-15.

Offensively for Batesville, Brooklynn Wilhelm set the offense with a team-high 10 assists. Madelyn Dierckman dished out three assists. Katie Wilhelm added two assists. Madelyn Haskamp and Elsie Kohne both had one assist.

Haskamp finished with a team-high six kills. Raegan Hixson added five kills. Brooklynn Wilhelm put away four kills. Molly Gesell finished with three kills and Koehne had one kill. Brooklynn Wilhelm served two aces while Dierckman and Caitlyn Fox both served one ace.

Defensively, Haskamp, Jelinek, Hixson and Koehne all had two blocks. Dierckmann led the Lady Bulldogs with 12 digs. Brooklynn Wilhelm finished with nine digs. Abby Miller tallied eight digs. Katie Wilhelm and Gesell both had three digs. Fox added two digs and Jelinek picked up one dig.

Tri-West Hendricks won the semistate title and will face West Noble for the Class 3A state championship.
