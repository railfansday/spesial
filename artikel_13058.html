so why not just invest into after tax right now instead of funding the 401k any more?
Because once you retire you want to make use of the standard deduction and probably the lower tax brackets. The same reason anyone uses traditional 401k, except you have even more reason to do so (not less) because you'll retire early: tax arbitrage. Unless your 401k balance (and I would separate HSA because that can come out tax free) is so high that your marginal rate is at least as high as now throughout retirement, I'd keep putting in more. Those planning on early retirement have more reason to use traditional retirement accounts, not less, as you have more future years of standard deduction to fill up.
