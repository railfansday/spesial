Gravenberch to miss Liverpool’s match against Fenerbahçe

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Injury has ruled Liverpool midfielder Ryan Gravenberch out of the Champions League match against Fenerbahçe in Istanbul.

The Netherlands international, who was an unused substitute in Sunday's Premier League defeat to Arsenal, was left behind on Merseyside to have treatment on a heel problem when the club flew out to Turkey on Tuesday morning.

Head coach Andoni Iraola has travelled with a 22-man squad as he seeks to win a fourth successive game in the competition.
