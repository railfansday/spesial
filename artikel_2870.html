Patriots waive S Elliott Davison

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Patriots waived safety Elliott Davison, the team announced Monday.

Davison, 24, signed with the Patriots' active roster from the practice squad on Oct. 19. He signed with the team's practice squad on Sept. 3 before being released on Sept. 14 and re-signed a day later.

He was elevated from the practice squad for two games and totaled two tackles on defense and one on special teams. Davison also appeared in the game against the Bears but had no stats.

He has played 10 defensive snaps and 57 on special teams.

Davison entered the NFL as an undrafted free agent, signing with the Saints in 2025. He spent last season on the Saints' practice squad and went to training camp with New Orleans this summer.

The Patriots also announced they signed running back Terrell Jennings to the practice squad.
