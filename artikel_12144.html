Milwaukee Brewers: Jacob Misiorowski competition for 2026 NL Cy Young revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Back in 2025, the Milwaukee Brewers made the decision to promote their top pitching prospect, Jacob Misiorowski, to the Major League level. This move led one former Brewers pitcher, Aaron Civale, to demand a trade as he was going to lose his spot in the starting rotation.

Since then, Misiorowski has made two All-Star rosters, set multiple pitch velocity records, became the ace of the staff, and won the World Series. Civale has played for three different teams, primarily as a reliever.

Oh, and Civale netted Milwaukee first baseman Andrew Vaughn.

Aug 21, 2026; Milwaukee, Wisconsin, USA; Milwaukee Brewers starting pitcher Jacob Misiorowski (32) throws a pitch in the first inning against the Atlanta Braves at American Family Field. Mandatory Credit: Benny Sieu-Imagn Images

Late in the 2025 season, though, the Brewers had to move Misiorowski to the bullpen. He was becoming fatigued as he pitched deeper into the season than he had ever done, and his pitches were not moving as well.

Despite this move, Misiorowski performed well in the 2025 postseason. However, many wondered if he would be able to build up the endurance needed to start over the course of an entire Major League season.

He began the 2026 season as the ace of Milwaukee's staff and soon proved to everyone that not only could he make a full season's worth of starts, he would be one of the best pitchers in the game while doing it. Now, he is the favorite to win the NL Cy Young Award, a notion made official when the finalist were announced on Monday night:

Misiorowski finished the regular season 16-5 with a 1.80 ERA (led the Majors), 252 strikeouts (led the Majors), 0.80 WHIP (led the Majors), and .157 opponent batting average (the best of all time).

Chris Sale and Cristopher Sanchez have been in the Cy Young conversation all season long, but the last few months have seen the Brewers ace pull away from them.

At this point, it is Misiorowski's award to win and a two-man race for second place.
