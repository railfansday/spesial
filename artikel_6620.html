Apple Butter Snack Cake

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Recipe by Margaret Knoebel, Milwaukee, Wisconsin

Made with apples and apple butter, this easy fall cake is just the thing when you need a sweet snack.

I know I’ve fallen into the autumnal magic of a fall farmer’s market when I walk away with too many apples, a jar of apple butter and no real plan for either. Luckily, our recipe tester Maggie Knoebel figured out how to combine both spoils into one super easy bake: an apple butter snack cake, complete with a brown butter glaze and cinnamon-sugar topping.

Preheat the oven to 350°F. In a large bowl, use a hand mixer or stand mixer to beat the eggs, sugar, apple butter, canola oil and vanilla extract.

In a separate bowl, whisk together the all-purpose flour, cinnamon, baking soda and salt. Add the flour mixture to the egg mixture and stir just until no streaks of flour remain. Fold in the apples and pecans.

Pour the batter into a greased 13×9-inch baking dish. Bake the cake until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean, 50 to 55 minutes. Cool the cake on a wire rack to room temperature.

Meanwhile, in a small, heavy saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat. Cook the butter until the milk solids are golden brown, stirring constantly, about 5 to 7 minutes. Immediately pour the brown butter into a mixing bowl to stop the cooking process or the butter will burn. Let the brown butter cool to room temperature.

Editor’s Tip: At some point during cooking, the butter will start to foam up. Don’t panic, it’s completely normal! Continue to stir while cooking, making sure to keep an eye on the butter’s color so you know when to take it off the heat.

Use a hand mixer or stand mixer to beat in the confectioners’ sugar until it’s fully combined with the brown butter. Spread the brown butter over the cake, then sprinkle the cinnamon-sugar mixture over the top before serving, if desired. Enjoy!

Store your apple butter snack cake in an airtight container in the fridge for up to four days. You really want to keep it well covered, so the glaze doesn’t absorb lingering fridge smells. If you want to keep the cake in the pan, cover the pan with storage wrap.

Yes, you can freeze apple butter snack cake. If you’re freezing the cake unfrosted, allow it to cool completely to room temperature, then cover the cake in storage wrap followed by aluminum foil. To freeze the glazed cake, place it in the freezer, uncovered, until the glaze is hard, about 30 minutes. Cover the cake with storage wrap and aluminum foil. Freeze the cake for up to two months and thaw overnight in the fridge.

Yes, you can bake an apple butter snack cake in a different-sized pan. Instead of a 13×9-inch baking dish, divide the batter evenly between two 8-inch square or two 9-inch cake pans. The baking time and temperature should be the same.

Yes, you can absolutely make this apple butter snack cake gluten- and/or dairy-free! Replace the all-purpose flour with a good gluten-free flour blend—one that’s designed as a cup-for-cup (or measure-for-measure) replacement. The cake is naturally dairy-free, but you’ll need to replace the butter in the glaze with vegan butter. You won’t be able to brown the vegan butter, so take 2 tablespoons off the measurement. That’s about how much liquid evaporates from butter when it’s browned.
