The Bruins will look to carry momentum from an overtime victory into Monday night's matchup against the Golden Knights at TD Garden.

Boston rallied from a two-goal third-period deficit Saturday to defeat Chicago, snapping a two-game losing streak. Mason Lohrei and Morgan Geekie scored late to force overtime before Elias Lindholm netted the game-winner.

The Bruins (10-4-1) will try to build on that performance as they open November against a Vegas team that enters the matchup at 9-5-1.
