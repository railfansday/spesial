Brittany Snow and Ex-Husband Tyler Stanaland’s Relationship Timeline

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Brittany Snow and Tyler Stanaland kept their relationship relatively private prior to their 2022 split.

“I love being married,” Snow exclusively told Us Weekly in December 2021. “It’s not any different, but being married in a pandemic is really like the test of everything if we can get through that, we’re good to go. If anything, it was the ultimate honeymoon because we were just staring at each other for six months, just here it is, you know?”

Snow, who began dating the Selling the OC personality in 2018, further opened up to Us about their then-private romance.

“I don’t really like to talk about personal stuff because I find that it’s a little strange to talk about my husband when he’s not here in that way,” Snow told Us at the time. “But I do love being married [and] I love him and we had a really great time together in terms of quarantine.”

Stanaland proposed to the Hunting Wives actress in 2019 after one year of dating and got married in March 2020. Snow and Stanaland confirmed two years later in September 2022 that they had split, with Snow officially filing for divorce the following January.

Snow and Stanaland’s divorce was finalized in July 2023, two years before the reality TV realtor moved on with Hannah Morrissey. (Stanaland proposed to Morrissey in October 2025 after less than one year of dating.)

Snow, meanwhile, moved on with photographer Hunter Moreno in 2024. Us confirmed the pair’s engagement in November 2025.
