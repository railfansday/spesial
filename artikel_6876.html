Katie Holmes and Jason Bard Yarmosky Look Wildly in Love as They Hit the Red Carpet at the American Ballet Theatre Fall Gala

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The actress was elegant in a green Kallmeyer dress

The David H. Koch Theater in Lincoln Center was filled with love last night as Katie Holmes and Jason Bard Yarmosky arrived hand-in-hand at the American Ballet Theatre Fall Gala.

The actor was was all minimalist elegance in a slate-green jersey dress with a rounded neckline, long sleeves, and a draped floor-length skirt. The piece hails from It girl-approved New York label Kallmeyer.

In true night-at-the-ballet style, Holmes accessorized only with pointy black pumps and a black shoulder bag made of tassels that swished back and forth as the Dawson’s Creek alum walked. Little diamond hoop earrings gave some shine to the look, while a glossy burgundy lip added a pop of contrast next to the green gown.

Her visual artist beau dressed up in a navy suit with a slightly oversize double-breasted blazer. He stayed true to his casual New Yorker style by pairing the sharp set with a classic black T-shirt and brown leather loafers.

The actor and the artist began dating earlier this year, and in the past several months, have attended many a public event together, always looking smitten in coordinating fits.

Last night’s gala honored Cynthia Erivo. The Wicked star was celebrated “for her remarkable contributions to the performing arts and her extraordinary capacity to inspire through multidisciplinary storytelling,” the ABT said. And for her big night, she wowed in a sculptural Erdem gown dripping with flowers.

Others in attendance included Nicky Hilton, Lake Bell (with Rashid Johnson), Ben Platt, and American ballerina Misty Copeland.
