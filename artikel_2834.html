Lady Pirates compete at state as a team for first time

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

It has been a great season if your team is competing in the final event of the campaign – the state finals. The Lady Pirates and Batesville's Jayla Bedel did just that.

The LaVern Gibson Championship Course in Terre Haute was the host for the IHSAA cross country state finals Saturday. This the first time the Lady Pirates have qualified for the state finals as a team.

Lake Central won the team title followed by Homestead and Carmel. Greensburg finished 25th in the team competition.

Daphne Weller of Fort Wayne Concordia was the individual champion with a state record setting time of 16:52.20.

Greensburg's Eleanor Davis raced in her second consecutive state meet. Davis placed 184th running 19:53.3 for 5000m. Davis won five different races in 2026. She also earned all-sectional and all-regional honors again this season. She had the second fastest time for a sophomore in school history with her season best performance of 18:49.9 at the Duke Meyer Classic.

Maria Scholle lowered her personal best Saturday as she crossed the finish line 20:13.6 for 206th place. Scholle concludes her freshman year with the 12th fastest time ever at Greensburg.

Lulu Webb was the next finisher for the Lady Pirates. She placed 211th in 20:22.5. Lulu ends her sophomore season earning all-conference and all-sectional for the second year in a row. She also has the fourth best time ever for sophomores with her season best time of 19:53.0.

Josie Slaven and Alli Bedel worked together throughout the state finals race and had personal best performances. Slaven crossed the line in 21:07.3 for 230th with Bedel next in 21:07.9.

Alaina Bedel ended her cross country career for the Lady Pirates at the state finals. Bedel placed 239th in 21:45.5. Over the last four seasons, Bedel has been All-EIAC, all-sectional, and all-regional as well as an individual qualifier for the state championships in cross country. Her personal best of 19:46.2 ranks seventh in school history.

Emma O'Sullivan was the seventh runner for the Lady Pirates. She ran 25:32.3 for 5km and placed 250th.

The Lady Pirates finished the 2026 season with a record of 110-44. Greensburg won the Lou Knoble Classic at Madison, Columbus North's Rick Weinhemier Classic, the Duke Meyer Classic at Hanover, and the Greensburg Invitational. The girls are also the first team ever to have over 100 wins in a season and qualify for the state meet.

Batesville junior Jayla Bedel also earned the right to compete at the state finals. Bedel ran a time of 19:21.27 which placed her 118th in the field.
