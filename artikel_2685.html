India's ODI record in New Zealand: Matches, wins and highest scores ahead of IND vs. NZ ODI series

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

India begin their five-match ODI series in New Zealand at Eden Park on November 4, after capturing the T20I series 3-2.

New Zealand won the last ODI series between the sides 2-1 in India in January while India have not won an ODI in New Zealand since February 2019.

Shubman Gill captains India in the first three ODIs, with Shreyas Iyer taking over for the last two, while Rohit Sharma and Virat Kohli return to the side. Mitchell Santner leads New Zealand, who have also named split squads.

India last toured New Zealand for ODIs in November 2022, when they lost in Auckland, and the next two games ended without a result because of rain.

Before the series starts in Auckland on Wednesday, The Sporting News takes a look at India's ODI record in New Zealand.

India have won 14 of their 45 ODIs in New Zealand and lost 26, while one was tied and four produced no result. Their last win came in Wellington in February 2019, and they have lost four and had two washouts in their six ODIs there since.

India hold the record with 392 for four in Christchurch in March 2009, which set up a 58-run win. Hamilton in February 2020 produced two of the top three totals, as New Zealand chased down India's 347 for four with 11 balls to spare.

All five of the lowest totals in these matches belong to India. Their 92 in Hamilton in January 2019 is the lowest, and the other four all came during the 2002-03 tour. New Zealand's lowest total at home against India is 142 in Auckland in March 1994.

Sachin Tendulkar's unbeaten 163 in Christchurch in 2009 is the highest score in these matches, and it came in India's record total. Tom Latham's 145 not out in Auckland in 2022 is New Zealand's best, while Virender Sehwag's 125 not out off 74 balls in Hamilton is third.

Trent Boult's 5 for 21 in Hamilton in 2019 is the best return in these matches, and it bowled India out for 92. Anil Kumble's 5 for 33 in Wellington in 1994 remains the best by an India bowler in New Zealand.

Across all India vs. New Zealand ODIs, Kohli leads with 1,897 runs at 57.48, including seven hundred, and he needs 103 more to reach 2,000 against them. Rohit is seventh with 1,134 runs, and 24 more would take him past Virender Sehwag.

Javagal Srinath leads the wicket-takers in these ODIs with 51, well clear of Anil Kumble on 39. Mohammed Shami and Tim Southee share third place with 38, although Shami needed only 16 matches and took a record 7 for 57 at the Wankhede in 2023.

Kuldeep Yadav is the leading active bowler in either squad with 27 wickets in 16 matches, and seven more would take him past Kapil Dev into the top five. Matt Henry, who plays the first three ODIs for New Zealand, has 21 wickets in 11 matches.
