46-year-old expected to succeed Nuno Espirito Santo as West Ham head coach

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

West Ham have sacked Nuno Espirito Santo after back-to-back Championship losses. WHUFC supporters made their feelings known with boos at half time and full time against West Brom on Saturday, and the 3-1 defeat has proven to be the Portuguese boss’ final match in charge.

The Hammers look to be moving quickly to name his replacement. The Athletic reports that 46-year-old Scott Parker is widely expected to succeed Nuno at the London Stadium with a deal in place between the club and the coach.

The hope is that Parker will be in place to lead West Ham against Cardiff City on Wednesday. Mark Noble, who is leading preparations on a caretaker basis, could serve as part of his backroom team.

Parker is someone all West Ham fans are familiar with. During his playing days, the former midfielder spent four years on the books with the Hammers, serving as captain and starring in the middle of the park before a move to Spurs came calling.

As a manager, he has built a strong reputation in the Championship. He has won promotions with Fulham, AFC Bournemouth and Burnley, the latter two being via the automatic promotion spots.

Parker’s style of play as a manager is defence-focused. His sides are set up to be compact and controlling despite not having possession, and this has yielded great success in the Championship. In the 2024/25 season, Burnley finished 2nd in the league with just 16 goals conceded – the fewest goals conceded by any side in the league’s history.

His track record in the Championship and close affiliation with the club and fans appears to be a recipe for success.

Parker’s past in the dugout suggests he has what it takes to deliver the promotion West Ham need this season. He’s a smart appointment on that front, and because of his bond with supporters who are crying out for a project they can really get behind.

However, should WHUFC return to the Premier League, Parker will have questions to answer. His time as a top-flight boss has not been as successful, losing 52 of his 86 matches in charge in England’s premier division.

The Londoner will be adamant he can deliver success in the Premier League, and determined to prove that. Those questions will only be answered in the long term though, and only if he can lead the Hammers back to the top flight first.
