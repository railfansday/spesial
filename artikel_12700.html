CACI Advances U.S. Air Force Financial Transparency and Mission Readiness

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

RESTON, Va., Nov. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- CACI International Inc (NYSE: CACI) announced today it has been awarded a five-year, $108 million contract by the U.S. Air Force to advance financial transparency, operational efficiency, and mission readiness. By sustaining and modernizing the Defense Enterprise Accounting and Management System (DEAMS), CACI enables the Air Force to make faster, data-driven decisions and maintain global financial operations with greater precision.

"Our technology-first approach delivers advanced automation and decision-quality data that help the Air Force operate more efficiently and maintain mission readiness," said John Mengucci, CACI President and Chief Executive Officer. "By applying secure, scalable technology, we help the Air Force strengthen financial transparency, improve decision-making, and modernize critical financial operations."

Through DEAMS modernization, CACI is helping the Air Force streamline financial operations, increase efficiency, and improve visibility across the enterprise. Building on DEAMS' recent unqualified audit opinion, CACI applies Agile delivery, automated testing, robust cybersecurity, and cloud-enabled operations to advance modernization and better support for the warfighter.
