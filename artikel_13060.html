Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: Teaching children about high deductible health plans

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

It’s my thinking that with most things in life you will get punished financially for chronically making decisions that lead to more dependency on others or institutions. I think it’s over the general public’s head when one starts talking about “triple tax advantaged”, investing instead of using HSA money (but saving big ticket receipts just in case) and paying out of pocket for any services you can afford.
It's also not appropriate for the general public to do this. Saving receipts for later reimbursement is only the right strategy if you are maxing out your tax-advantaged accounts, which isn't common. And it is even less common for investors who are starting out with an HSA, who are likely to be early in their careers and thus to have lower salaries and debt payments which limit their savings.

The most important general lesson is that you should max out the HSA in preference to any other investment (except a 401(k) with an employer match); the HSA is a tax-free account with a match from the IRS.
