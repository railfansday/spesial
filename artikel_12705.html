100% Bearing Free: Maglev Enables 6-DOF with Zero Contact, Applications in Hybrid Bonding, Wafer Metrology

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Three technology demonstrators, a 400mm linear stage, a rotary wafer stage, and a picometer-class AFM scanner, correct motion errors electronically with no bearing contact, lubrication, or wear

SHREWSBURY, Mass., Nov. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- PI (Physik Instrumente) displayed a variety of high-precision motion systems based on magnetic levitation technology, at SEMICON West 2026. This advanced technology enables active six-axis control, without any restrictions from traditional bearings. Each stage levitates and directly actuates its moving platform, eliminating mechanical bearing contact, lubrication, and bearing wear. Target applications include hybrid bonding and advanced packaging, wafer inspection, and high-resolution surface metrology.

It supports a constant-velocity Z approach with contact detection, then applies process forces for bonding above 100N with mN force resolution. The stage achieves 2g acceleration and 400mm/s; other configurations support up to 6g.

Rotary Wafer Stage: Unlimited Rotation with Active Plane ControlThe rotary demonstrator pairs unlimited Î¸Z travel and speeds above 600rpm with active control of the rotor center and plane. Axial position and tip/tilt are stabilized throughout rotation, holding axial error below 5nm and tilt error below 50nrad peak-to-peak to maintain the measurement plane during wafer inspection and metrology. Payloads up to 25kg are possible with other configurations.

AFM Scanner: Picometer Resolution over Millimeter RangesThe MagLev AFM scanner combines piezo-class resolution, 50pm or better in X/Y and 100pm or better in Z, with 12.1mm of X/Y travel, supporting die-level profiling without stitching. Other configurations can extend X/Y travel to 50mm.

Why Active Six-Axis Control MattersMagnetic levitation nanopositioning systems replace mechanical bearings with actively controlled magnetic fields, eliminating friction, wear, lubrication, and bearing-related particle generation. Active six-axis control also allows positioning and alignment errors to be corrected electronically within a single motion platform, reducing the need to stack multiple mechanical positioning stages.

Award-Winning MagLev Poster at SEMICON West 2026PI's MagLev and active wafer chuck technologies were also presented and received the best poster award at the SEMICON West Test Vision Poster Session, under the title "Maglev Platforms and Active Wafer Chuck Technology for Advanced Semiconductor Manufacturing." The work addresses next-generation hybrid bonding, inspection, and metrology applications where cleanliness, positioning accuracy, and throughput are increasingly important.

Applications and Industries Served  Semiconductor manufacturing, advanced packaging and hybrid bonding, wafer inspection, high-resolution surface metrology, optics and photonics.
