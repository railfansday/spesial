Scotland draw Germany, Netherlands & Sweden

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Scotland will meet Germany, Netherlands and Sweden in the next edition of the women's Nations League.

Melissa Andreatta's side are preparing for a World Cup play-off with the Swedes in December.

Scotland, who were in the lowest tier of seeds in the A division, met Germany and the Dutch in the 2025 competition.

They lost 6-1 and 4-0 to Germany, but the games against Netherlands were much closer, with a 2-1 home defeat and a 1-1 draw in Tilburg.

Scotland lost 4-0 and 1-0 to the Dutch in 2023.

Sweden and Scotland last met in a 2021 friendly in Paisley, with the visitors prevailing 2-0.
