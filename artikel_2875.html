Falcons rule DE Samson Ebukam, WR Zachariah Branch out for Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Falcons have ruled out a pair of players for this week’s game against the Bengals in Madrid.

Head coach Kevin Stefanski said that defensive end Samson Ebukam and wide receiver Zachariah Branch will both miss the game due to hamstring injuries. Stefanski said he does not know if the two players will miss more time.

Ebukam has 11 tackles and 2.5 sacks in seven appearances this season. Branch has 10 catches for 64 yards on offense and he has also served as a punt and kickoff returner.

The Falcons will update the rest of their injuries when they release their first practice report of the week on Wednesday.
