Bears superfan Ashton Kutcher ‘fights’ Blitz before Seahawks takedown

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Before the Monday Night Football game between the Chicago Bears and the Seattle Seahawks, actor Ashton Kutcher fought the 12's mascot, Blitz.

Suffice it to say, he won. A video shows Kutcher holding Blitz down, and Blitz grabs the actor's leg. The That 70s Show star turned it into an armbar, forcing the mascot to tap out.

They exchanged a few more words as Seahawks employees tried to make Blitz walk away. This was a fun moment before the exciting MNF game.

The Monday Night Football game between the Bears and the Seahawks started slow. Chicago was up 7-0 at the end of the first quarter, and they only led 13-9 at halftime.

Things picked up in the fourth quarter. Jason Myers had a 33-yard field goal blocked by the Bears, but Chicago answered by fumbling it back to the Seahawks.

It only took Sam Darnold three plays to answer back, throwing a seven-yard touchdown to Cooper Kupp. The Bears answered, as D'Andre Swift took the offense down the field to score another touchdown. Swift finished the game with just 69 yards while averaging 2.6 yards per carry. He scored three touchdowns, though.

Darnold then threw another interception, and Swift scored another touchdown on the following drive. The Seahawks' offense turned it over on downs, and the Bears ran out the clock.

This was a sobering loss for the Seahawks, who were a week removed from blowing out the Kansas City Chiefs on Sunday Night Football. They drop to .500 heading into a game against the Arizona Cardinals, who just upset the Dallas Cowboys at AT&T Stadium.

The Bears improve to 6-2 and are in second place in the NFC North. They have won three straight before their Sunday Night Football game against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers.
