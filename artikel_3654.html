Grace Ann Nader and Rebecca Minkoff Debut 'Playful' and 'Edgy' Fashion Collab

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Leave your baggage behind and pick up something worth carrying because these two fashion-forward friends are bringing their personal styles together for a new three-piece capsule collection.

Fashion designer Rebecca Minkoff and Love Thy Nader star Grace Ann Nader have teamed up on a limited-edition line that features a cropped bomber jacket, black suede boots and a leather hobo bag. The collection, which launched Monday, November 2, is Nader’s first fashion collaboration where she isn’t just modeling Minkoff’s designs — she’s helping create them.

“Working with Rebecca on this collection has been such an exciting experience!” Nader exclusively told Us. “It gave me the opportunity to be involved in the creative process in a completely new way and design pieces from scratch, which I’ve always wanted to do.”

But before the two were exchanging design ideas, Nader was Minkoff’s plus-one to the 2025 CFDA Awards. Minkoff later cast the model in her spring 2026 campaign and as their friendship grew, the pair found a way to bring their individual takes on cool-girl chic into one collection.

“I’ve always loved fashion and using it to express my personality,” Nader told Us.” I wanted these pieces to feel like a true reflection of my personal style — a little edgy, a little playful, but effortless and wearable.”

For those looking to put their best foot forward, the Carson Boot ($239) is a very special combination of the Minkoff and Nader’s aesthetics with a velvety black ruched suede and edgy beaded accents.

The Grace Ann Cropped Jacket ($248) starts with a classic black bomber silhouette that trades a traditional collar for a luxe contrasting brown faux fur trim, a flattering cropped fit and a button-front closure that lets the textures do all the talking.

The “Holland” Nolita Hobo bag pays a special homage to the model-turned-reality star’s mother, Holland — featuring a sleek yet slouchy suede traditional silhouette with retro silver hardware, a detachable strap and plenty of pocket space for necessities.

The collection is now available on Rebecca Minkoff’s website as a limited release, and Nader is already looking forward to seeing the pieces come to life and find space in others’ wardrobes.

“Rebecca has been such an incredible friend, and collaborator, and I’m so proud of what we created together,” Nader said. “I can’t wait to see how everyone makes these pieces their own!”
