Matt Rhule reveals Nebraska fan verbally attacked his daughter

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Nebraska Cornhuskers lost their third straight game, falling 17-10 to Washington on Saturday, and head coach Matt Rhule dedicated his Monday press conference to defending his program.

Rhule spoke uninterrupted for 19 minutes before opening his presser to questions. In that time, he analyzed what went wrong against Washington, credited individual coaches and players, including offensive coordinator Dana Holgorsen and quarterback Anthony Colandrea, for their effort, and pulled back the curtain on how the college football pressure cooker spilled over into his personal life.

"There's so many people that you do not want to let down," Rhule said. "I do not want to let the fans down. I don't want to let our players down, I don't want to let our coaches down, but I got to go to work. What I will say, though, is this: I'm also going to fight for my family."

"My kids have grown up in coaching—and I say this for all the coaches out there," Rhule continued. "I love our fans. But a guy walked up to my 13-year-old daughter and was like, 'F you and F your dad.' I'm not going to keep coaching if it means my kids grow up screwed up. I'm not going to [keep] coaching to see my daughter shook."

Rhule emphasized that he's willing to "fight" for the die-hard fans who don't cross inappropriate boundaries.

"The guy that attacked my 13-year-old daughter, I'm not coaching for him," Rhule said. "I'm coaching for the people that are in the boat. So, you guys can ask me whatever you want, man, but just please don't question anybody's integrity or effort."

Rhule doubled down on his commitment to Nebraska and its "passionate" fan base—the "grown man" who harassed his daughter notwithstanding.

"I'm not leaving, I'm not quitting, I'm fighting for those people," he said.

Rhule also revealed what his 10-year-old daughter texted him.

"She was like, 'Mom had the question with us, like, is this too much for you guys?'" Rhule said. "She was like, 'Dad, I love Nebraska, I love living in Nebraska, I love Nebraska football, [and] I love the players. I'm tough. This is just teaching me life lessons. Please don't quit.' So, how do you keep the team motivated? You know what I'm going to say to them? Don't quit."

The Cornhuskers named Rhule their head football coach and signed him to an eight-year contract in November 2022, shortly after the Carolina Panthers fired him. Rhule previously coached Temple and Baylor at the college level. Nebraska is 24-22 under Rhule.

Nebraska (5-3) will visit the Illinois Fighting Illini (5-3) on Friday, Nov. 6.
