Lions provide update on LB Jack Campbell

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Lions have provided an update regarding the condition of linebacker Jack Campbell, who suffered a neck injury during Sunday’s game against the Vikings.

“Jack Campbell continues to rest and recover at a local hospital,” the team said. “Preliminary indications are positive and out of respect for Jack and his family we will not share further information at this time.”

Campbell was taken to the hospital following the fourth-quarter collision that left him motionless. He was immobilized and transported off the field.

At the hospital, Campbell has been treated for his neck injury. The team has provided few specifics at this point. Campbell remained hospitalized overnight.
