Fantasy Basketball Top 200 Rankings: Reason for concern for Shai Gilgeous-Alexander?

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Should fantasy managers who drafted Oklahoma City Thunder guard Shai Gilgeous-Alexander be concerned? While the league's reigning Most Valuable Player is healthy, some of his key teammates couldn't avoid injuries that will sideline them for extended periods. As a result, SGA has been left to shoulder even more responsibility, leading a rotation that has become less experienced than it was on Opening Night.

First, the Thunder lost Cason Wallace (shoulder) and Jalen Williams (ankle/fibula) during the same game, an October 25 win over the Clippers, and they're not guaranteed to play again this season. That game is also the last time Chet Holmgren (knee) played, and he'll be out for at least 8-10 weeks. Lastly, Alex Caruso was sidelined by a sprained ankle suffered during an October 26 win over the Suns and has not played since.

In Oklahoma City's last three games, Gilgeous-Alexander has averaged 24.3 points, 4.3 rebounds, 2.7 assists, 0.7 steals, 0.7 blocks and 1.7 three-pointers per game, shooting 49.1 percent from the field and 85.7 percent from the foul line. Those certainly aren't "bad" numbers, but they aren't on par with what most fantasy managers expect from SGA when selecting within the first three or four picks of a draft.

Can he continue to produce at a level that would align with his preseason ADP? And do the injuries in Oklahoma City open the door for Lakers guard Luka Dončić to crack the expected top three of Gilgeous-Alexander, San Antonio's Victor Wembanyama and Denver's Nikola Jokić (in no particular order)? It will be interesting to see how things play out for SGA, including whether or not the Thunder use some of their future draft capital to improve the roster.
