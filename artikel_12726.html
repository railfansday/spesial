Gusto Small Business Jobs and Pay Report: Small Businesses Add 38,000 Jobs in October, Marking Ninth Straight Month of Growth

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Hiring Picks Up for a Second Straight Month and Tops the 12-Month Average

SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- America's small businesses added an estimated 38,000 net new jobs in October on a seasonally adjusted basis, according to the latest Gusto Small Business Jobs and Pay Report, marking the ninth consecutive month of positive hiring. Hiring picked up for a second straight month and came in above the 12-month average of 34,000. October picked up from September, which was revised down to 27,000 net new jobs (from an initial estimate of 28,000).

Hiring was broad across sectors, regions, and business size: 16 of 19 sectors, all four U.S. regions, and every company size tier added jobs in October. Health Care and Social Assistance again led all sectors with 13,000 net hires, followed by Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services (+5,500) and Construction (+3,800). Average Hourly Earnings across small business employees rose 3.9% year-over-year to $37.50, while starting pay for newly hired workers climbed 3.0% to about $25.90 an hour.

"October was a step up in pace, but not quite a return to the pace we saw in spring," said Nich Tremper, Senior Economist at Gusto. "Hiring picked up for the second month in a row and came in above the 12-month average. We saw growth across all business sizes and in 16 of 19 sectors, and pay kept rising, with average hourly earnings up 3.9% from a year ago."

At +38,000, October's net hires came in above the 12-month average of +34,000, the largest gain since July after a softer couple of months. Recent months: June (+36,000), July (+40,000), August (+25,000), September (+27,000). Prior months were revised with this release: September was revised down to +27,000 (from +28,000), and August was revised up to +25,000 (from +18,500). As always, the most recent months are subject to revision as additional payroll data arrives.
